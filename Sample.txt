==============  Example 1 (Standard NDA & Liability) ===========

CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-DISCLOSURE The Receiving Party acknowledges that during the term of this Agreement, it may acquire knowledge of or have access to proprietary information or trade secrets of the Disclosing Party. The Receiving Party shall hold all such confidential information in strict confidence and must not disclose, distribute, or disseminate the information to any third party without prior written consent. The Receiving Party will restrict access to confidential information only to its employees who have a strict need to know.

LIMITATION OF LIABILITY IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE TO THE OTHER FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, SPECIAL, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES (INCLUDING, WITHOUT LIMITATION, DAMAGES FOR LOSS OF BUSINESS PROFITS, BUSINESS INTERRUPTION, OR LOSS OF DATA) ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES. The total cumulative liability of the Service Provider under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client in the three (3) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

TERMINATION Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, by providing thirty (30) days prior written notice to the other Party. Furthermore, the Disclosing Party may immediately terminate this Agreement in the event of a material breach of the confidentiality obligations set forth herein by the Receiving Party. Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement, the Receiving Party must promptly return or securely destroy all documents and media containing the Disclosing Party's confidential information.

====================  Example 4 (Commercial Lease Agreement) ====================================

PREMISES AND AS-IS CONDITION The Landlord agrees to lease the commercial space located at 123 Main Street to the Tenant for a period of five (5) years. The Tenant accepts the premises in its "AS-IS" condition. The Landlord makes no warranties, express or implied, regarding the suitability of the premises for the Tenant's intended business operations.

MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS The Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, keep and maintain the entire premises in good working order, including but not limited to the HVAC system, plumbing, electrical systems, roof, and structural foundations. If the HVAC system requires replacement during the lease term, the Tenant shall bear the full cost of such replacement.

LATE CHARGES AND DEFAULT If any rent payment is not received by the Landlord within three (3) days of the due date, the Tenant shall pay a late fee equal to 10% of the overdue amount, plus interest accruing at a rate of 1.5% per month. The Landlord may terminate this lease and initiate eviction proceedings if rent remains unpaid for five (5) days.

====================  Example 5 (Draconian Employment Agreement) ====================================

AT-WILL EMPLOYMENT Your employment with OmniCorp is "at-will." This means that either you or the Company may terminate your employment at any time, with or without cause, and with or without advance notice.

LIQUIDATED DAMAGES FOR EARLY DEPARTURE In consideration of the extensive training provided by the Company, you agree that if you voluntarily resign from your position within the first twenty-four (24) months of employment, you shall pay the Company the sum of $25,000 as liquidated damages, which the Company may deduct from your final paycheck.

NON-SOLICITATION For a period of thirty-six (36) months following the termination of your employment for any reason, you agree not to directly or indirectly solicit, hire, or attempt to hire any current or former employee, contractor, or vendor of the Company, nor shall you solicit any current or prospective customer of the Company for the purpose of providing competing services.
